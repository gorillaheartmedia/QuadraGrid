Traffic Light Model

Green: goal is on course. Usually completed recently and not falling behind.
Yellow: goal is slipping. Missed a day or showing inconsistent completion.
Red: goal has failed to complete over a consistent period of days or is seriously behind.

In this build, green = 70%+ completion rate and no long miss streak, yellow = 40%–69% or a short miss streak, red = below 40% or 3+ missed days in a row.
